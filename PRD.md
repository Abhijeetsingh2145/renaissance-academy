# PRD --- Renaissance Academy Website

## 1. Product

Build a completely new website for Renaissance Academy, Gorakhpur (Bala
Par), replacing the existing website.

## 2. Goals

-   Generate new admission enquiries.
-   Present complete and accurate school information.
-   Improve the school's online presence.
-   Provide a professional digital experience for prospective parents.

## 3. Target Users

-   Prospective parents and guardians.
-   Existing parents.
-   Students.
-   General visitors.
-   Prospective staff.

## 4. Website Scope

The website must include:

-   Home
-   About School
-   Principal/Chairman/Director messages
-   Vision & Mission
-   Academics
-   Facilities
-   Sports & Activities
-   Achievements
-   Events/News
-   Gallery
-   Admissions
-   Careers
-   Contact
-   CBSE Mandatory Public Disclosure
-   Faculty/Teachers
-   School Rules/Policies
-   Fee Structure

The homepage must be selective and focused. Detailed information should
be available on dedicated inner pages rather than placing everything on
the homepage.

## 5. Admission System

The public admission enquiry form must collect:

-   Parent/Guardian Name
-   Student Name
-   Date of Birth
-   Class Seeking Admission
-   Phone Number
-   Email Address
-   Previous School

Submissions must be stored and viewable by the school through the
private admin dashboard.

A new submission must also trigger an email notification and the
approved WhatsApp notification mechanism.

## 6. Admin System

Use one admin account.

The admin dashboard must allow the authorized user to:

### Enquiries

-   View submitted admission enquiries.

### Notices

-   Create notices.
-   Add title and description.
-   Set an expiry date.
-   Publish or hide a notice.
-   Edit notices.
-   Delete notices.

Only published and non-expired notices should be displayed publicly.

## 7. Public Notice Board

Provide a notice-board control/popup on the right side of the website.

It should display currently published, non-expired notices.

The school must be able to manage notices without developer involvement.

## 8. WhatsApp

Provide a persistent WhatsApp contact button at the bottom-right of the
website.

The button must open a WhatsApp conversation with the school's
designated number.

The admission enquiry workflow must support WhatsApp notification to the
school through the selected implementation.

## 9. Content

Content creation is a shared responsibility between the school and the
project team.

The school will provide available institutional information and assets.
The project team will research, write, structure, edit, and creatively
present the content.

Available content/assets include:

-   School logo.
-   Brand colour.
-   School photographs and videos.
-   Principal, Chairman and Director information, messages and
    photographs.
-   Academic information.
-   Facilities information.
-   Admission information.
-   Fee information.
-   Achievements.
-   Events and activities.
-   CBSE disclosure information/documents.
-   School rules/policies.

Teacher information is not currently confirmed as available and must not
be invented.

## 10. Brand & Experience

The website must feel:

-   Premium.
-   Modern.
-   Trustworthy.
-   Academic.
-   Welcoming.

The existing school logo and its blue brand colour must be retained as
the basis of the visual identity.

The existing reference website may inform information breadth, but its
homepage should not be copied. The Renaissance Academy homepage must
remain focused and uncluttered.

## 11. Language

The website will be English-only.

## 12. SEO

The project includes full local SEO setup, including:

-   Technical SEO.
-   Keyword research.
-   Local search targeting.
-   On-page SEO.
-   Content optimization.
-   Google Business Profile considerations.
-   Google Search Console and indexing setup.

## 13. Success Criteria

The website is successful when:

-   Prospective parents can understand the school and its offering
    easily.
-   Admission information and enquiry submission are easy to access.
-   The school can independently view enquiries.
-   The school can independently manage public notices.
-   The website presents the school as a premium modern institution.
-   The website provides complete school information without overloading
    the homepage.
-   The website is ready for launch and local search visibility.
